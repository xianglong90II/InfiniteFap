Inference contract
- Input: 22050 Hz mono, arithmetic-mean channel downmix.
- Features: centered reflect-padded periodic-Hann STFT, n_fft=1024, hop=441; magnitude divided by sqrt(1024), projection through the supplied row-major [513,128] mel filterbank, then log1p(1000 * x).
- ONNX input: spect [1,1500,128]. Outputs: beat and downbeat logits [1,1500].
- Chunking: 1500 frames, 6-frame borders, 50 frames/sec. Each window is inferred independently and overlap is aggregated by keeping the first window's valid frames.
- Candidate beat/downbeat peaks use logit > 0 and a 7-frame local maximum. Downbeats are snapped to nearby detected beats.
